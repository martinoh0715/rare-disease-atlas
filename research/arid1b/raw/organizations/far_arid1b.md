# FAR — About ARID1B-RD

Source: https://www.arid1b.org/arid1b
Retrieved: 2026-10-03
Access: public website

Key passages used in curation:
- ARID1B variants are the most common cause of Coffin-Siris syndrome.
- Haploinsufficiency: one nonfunctional copy; remaining copy not enough.
- Typically de novo; rarely inherited.
- No treatment currently; supportive management.
- Epilepsy occurs in up to 30% of children affected by ARID1B-RD (advocacy page; retain as source-attributed).
