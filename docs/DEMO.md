# Demo walkthrough — HBB Evidence Atlas

Audience: patient-group leader exploring cross-community research connections.

Time: ~5 minutes locally at http://127.0.0.1:43123

## 1. Land on the atlas

- Confirm the brand **HBB Evidence Atlas** and the caution that exa-cel targets **BCL11A**, not HBB.
- Note curated vs discovery counts in the header.

## 2. Follow the guided journey

In the left rail, step through:

1. Sickle cell anemia (HbSS)
2. HbSS genotype / HbS allele / HBB
3. Fetal hemoglobin reactivation + BCL11A process
4. exa-cel intervention + PMID 38661449 (SCD trial)
5. Parallel PMID 38657265 (TDT trial)
6. Hypothesis bridge
7. SCDAA, Cooley’s Anemia Foundation, JAX Townes model
8. Next research question with uncertainties

## 3. Inspect evidence on a connection

- Click a solid teal edge (established) and a dashed pink edge (hypothesis).
- In the evidence panel, verify:
  - source URL
  - supporting passage
  - dates
  - genotype / species / experimental context
  - separate extraction confidence vs evidence strength
  - limitations (especially “not HBB editing”, animal ≠ human, SCD model ≠ β-thalassemia)

## 4. Ask the four user questions

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Which community has a biological connection? | Journey + disease nodes (HbSS / SCD broad / TDT / β-thalassemia) |
| What assets exist? | Resource panel (orgs + JAX model + publications) |
| Who could help? | SCDAA / CAF panels (no invented availability) |
| What next? | Research question + validation questions |

## 5. Stress-test honesty

- Search for nonsense text → “missing data / unsupported search” message.
- Toggle **Show discovery layer** → many unreviewed records appear; keep them out of default claims.
- Open ` /data/gaps.md` for remaining scientific/data gaps.

## Stop conditions / non-claims

- Do not present search hits as causal proof.
- Do not equate sickle cell trait with disease.
- Do not treat study status as efficacy.
- Do not recommend treatment.
