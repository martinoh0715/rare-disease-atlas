# Demo script (HBB + ARID1B)

## HBB baseline

1. Search **sickle cell anemia** or **HbSS**.
2. **Understand the connection** — follow HbSS → HbS/HBB → HbF/BCL11A → exa-cel → publications / TDT bridge.
3. **Explore resources** — open study and organization edges; note Townes model caveats.
4. **Evaluate research opportunity** — select a disease and research asset, optionally enter an objective, then prepare a collaboration brief from the assessment results.

Non-claim to say aloud: exa-cel edits BCL11A, not HBB. Organization pages identify potentially relevant groups; they do not verify collaborator networks or availability.

## ARID1B extension

1. Search **ARID1B** or **Coffin-Siris**.
2. Confirm the dataset badge switches to **ARID1B-related disorders**.
3. **Understand the connection** — ARID1B-RD → heterozygous genotype context → haploinsufficiency / BAF → CSS1 vs nonsyndromic ID kept distinct.
4. **Explore resources** — van der Sluijs 2019 spectrum paper, CARE4ARID1B natural history, FAR.
5. **Evaluate research opportunity** — assess a selected registry or publication against a community objective (not a treatment claim).

Honest gap to say aloud: no approved disease-modifying therapy; no invented link to HBB.

## Unsupported search

Try a term outside both curated slices. The page should explain missing evidence without claiming the biology does not exist.
