# Expansion candidate comparison

Candidate material came from the attached hackathon notes. Claims were checked against primary public sources before selection. Only one candidate is imported in this extension.

| Criterion | ARID1B-related disorders | GATA6-related disorders | ODC1-related disorders |
|---|---|---|---|
| Variant / mechanism evidence | Strong: heterozygous LoF / haploinsufficiency in SWI/SNF (BAF); large CSS↔ID spectrum cohort (van der Sluijs et al., PMID 31249371); ClinGen haploinsufficiency score 3 | Strong organ-development genetics (pancreatic agenesis + CHD), but mechanism is transcription-factor dosage across organs rather than a single well-packaged rare-disease research network in public pages | Clear **gain-of-function** C-terminal cluster for Bachmann–Bupp (BABS); **LoF disease association is unresolved** (GeneReviews) |
| Disease entities / phenotypes | CSS1 (OMIM 135900) kept distinct from broader ARID1B-RD / nonsyndromic ID; variable expressivity documented | Broad spectrum (neonatal diabetes → adult-onset; CHD rates high in case series) — harder to keep entities unmerged | BABS is well defined; cancer ODC1 upregulation must stay separate from germline monogenic disease |
| Orgs / investigators / studies | FAR (arid1b.org); CARE4ARID1B natural-history consortium (7 sites); published expert cohort work | No dedicated GATA6 patient foundation identified in quick source check; assets are mostly cohort papers / consortia fragments | Smaller case series; DFMO compassionate-use reports exist but are treatment-status sensitive |
| Complete demo journey | Yes: disease → haploinsufficiency/BAF → spectrum phenotypes → publication → natural-history study → FAR → next research question | Partial: biology yes; partner/asset path weaker for a second atlas slice | Partial for BABS; notes mix LoF speculation and oncology — high fabrication risk if imported wholesale |
| Tests capabilities beyond HBB | Autosomal-dominant **de novo** inheritance; haploinsufficiency (not recessive genotype structures); no approved gene-editing intervention; spectrum/variable expressivity; natural-history study as research asset | Multi-organ developmental spectrum | Gain-of-function + investigational repurposing — useful later, but LoF caveats conflict with notes |

## Selection

**Selected for first extension: ARID1B-related disorders.**

Reasons:
1. Reliable variant and haploinsufficiency evidence with explicit spectrum (CSS vs nonsyndromic ID).
2. Named disease entities with OMIM/Orphanet/MONDO anchors and a large reviewed cohort paper.
3. Verifiable patient organization (FAR) and multi-site natural-history study (CARE4ARID1B).
4. One complete sourced journey without inventing treatment efficacy.
5. Stress-tests non-HBB inheritance and genotype assumptions.

**Not selected now**
- **GATA6**: solid genetics, weaker public partner/asset surface for a full atlas journey.
- **ODC1**: BABS is real, but notes over-reach on LoF clustering and cancer expression; DFMO status must stay compassionate-use / investigational, not approval.

pLoF-clustering guidance (PMID 38671509) is treated as a caution against automatic pathogenicity rules — not imported as a universal edge generator.
